# The settings

The page at `/settings` and the phone's Settings screen, under no section.
The account menu opens it, as `../shell/README.md` gives.

- **The settings are a page of their own, `/settings`, as on the phone.** The
  page reads as the phone's Settings screen does: the account first, then
  the Study settings, the daily limit on new questions and the exam size.
  A reader of either client then finds a standing choice in the same place.
  Feedback comes before the record. The record's export and the account's
  deletion come last on both clients, since the deletion is the one act there
  that cannot be undone.
- **A study setting is a record of the user's log**, so a change on one client
  stands on the other. `../../../api/knowledge-log.md` gives the records. The
  device name and the reminder hour are about one phone, and stay on the
  phone's screen alone.
